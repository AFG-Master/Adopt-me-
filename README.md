# 🐾 ADOPT ME — Adopt Me money farm

Takes care of your pet's needs (and your own when you play as a baby) so you earn Bucks while AFK.

## ▶️ Load it
Paste into your executor while you're in Adopt Me:

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AFG-Master/Adopt-me-/main/adopt_me.lua"))()
```

**Auto-start:** copy [`autoexec/adopt_me.lua`](autoexec/adopt_me.lua) into your executor's *autoexec* folder. It only runs in Adopt Me and always loads the newest version.

## ✨ Features
- **Pet care:** Salon, School, Pizza Party, Cat Cafe, Hospital (doctor), Park, Campsite, Beach, weather spots, bed / bath / toilet in your house, food & drink, walk, ride (stroller), play, mystery needs
- **Baby mode:** on the Babies team it also does *your* needs — one trip pays you and your pet
- **Smart picking:** best pay first, groups tasks in the same place, walks while waiting, Task Board dailies first
- **Food:** uses your backpack, buys $1 apple / water when you run out (spending cap)
- **Stuck-pet swap:** a pet stuck on a need the script can't finish is swapped for another pet
- **Auto claims:** playtime paycheck CASH OUT, daily stars, dailies reward (free egg), idle progress, mailbox
- **AFK safety:** anti-AFK, auto rejoin, battery saver (3D off + low FPS)
- Small draggable panel with Bucks, tasks and $/hour; settings are saved
